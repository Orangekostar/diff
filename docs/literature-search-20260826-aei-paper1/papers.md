# Literature Search: AEI Paper 1 Scope and Structure

Date: 2026-08-26
Purpose: verify AEI scope fit, recent paper organization, and the closest
C-scan-to-CAI novelty boundary

## Main finding

The official AEI scope makes a generic sensing or machine-learning application
an insufficient contribution. The paper must expose an engineering-information
construct and show how it supports a knowledge-intensive decision task. The
Useful-Observable-Actionable hierarchy satisfies that requirement only when it
is presented as a falsifiable separation of task value, conditional inference,
and decision conversion, with adverse controls retained.

## Structural finding

Recent AEI application papers commonly separate framework/method from
validation, and several use a separate case-study or engineering-validation
section. A merged Results and Discussion section is compatible with the
evidence-chain narrative. Publisher access did not expose complete headings for
every paper; inaccessible structures are explicitly marked rather than
reconstructed from abstracts.

## Closest novelty boundary

Mack et al. directly predicts CAI strength and impact energy from C-scan images.
Therefore this paper cannot claim that using full C-scan morphology for CAI is
new. Its distinct contribution is the information hierarchy: task-relevant
information can be useful yet not conditionally observable, and even partially
observable value can fail to become actionable under valuation and set-planning
constraints.
