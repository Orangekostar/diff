# AEI Positive Progressive Narrative Design

## Objective

Replace the three-program Useful/Observable/Actionable result story with two
progressive scientific chains while keeping the 39 canonical claims and every
numerical direction unchanged.

## Considered approaches

### Prose-only rewrite

Changing only the title, abstract, and results headings would leave old figure
names, Table 3 groupings, tests, and package contracts inconsistent. It cannot
satisfy the prompt's traceability and deterministic-delivery requirements.

### Surface rename with existing evidence groupings

Renaming figures and sections while retaining the U/O/A row organization would
still make the old ladder the visual and validation architecture. It would also
leave O2 in the wrong scientific role and O1 as a failure headline.

### Integrated evidence-mapped refactor

Create a 39-row narrative map that assigns each canonical claim to exactly one
of 12 stages. Drive manuscript comments, Figure 1-4 sources, Table 3, semantic
validation, and packaging from that contract. This is the selected design
because it provides one checkable narrative authority without altering the
numeric authority.

## Scientific architecture

Part I characterizes task-relevant information as spatial, sparsely
recoverable, heterogeneous, objective-conditioned, state-conditioned, and
predictor-conditioned. Part II realizes that information through a legal causal
state, dynamic valuation, matched attribution controls, valuation/planning
decomposition, bounded set realization, and deployment calibration.

Usefulness, task-value observability, and actionability remain validation
criteria. They are not section-level success/failure programs.

## Data and ownership boundaries

`PAPER_CANONICAL_METRICS.csv` remains the sole numeric authority and is
byte-immutable. `PAPER_EVIDENCE_CHRONOLOGY.csv` remains the timing authority.
The new `PAPER_POSITIVE_NARRATIVE_MAP.csv` owns narrative placement only. Its
Markdown companion documents the mapping for human audit.

No result source, trained model, authority artifact, historical GO/NOGO record,
or P7 endpoint is modified. Derived figures, tables, PDFs, and package copies
are regenerated from code.

## Manuscript contract

The manuscript keeps six numbered sections and replaces three RQs with RQ-A
and RQ-B. Section 3 defines the two-part framework. Section 5.1 and 5.2 are the
only primary experimental parts, each with six ordered stages; Section 5.3 is
synthesis. The introduction has six paragraphs and the conclusion three.

Negative directions remain in local control/boundary sentences but cannot
dominate the title, abstract headline, contribution paragraph, headings, or
part syntheses.

## Visual contract

Figure 1 is the two-part framework, not a U/O/A ladder. Figure 2 carries Part I
spatial/sparse/heterogeneity/objective evidence. Figure 3 bridges state
evolution to dynamic valuation and source attribution. Figure 4 separates
valuation, planning, feedback, and the final deployable gap. Okabe-Ito colors,
markers/hatches, vector output, readable final-size text, and hash-bound source
rows are required.

Table 3 has exactly 12 stage rows. Each row presents the positive scientific
role and an adjacent control/boundary, preventing narrative reframing from
hiding adverse evidence.

## Verification contract

Tests must fail first on the old structure, then pass after implementation.
Verification covers stage assignment, canonical hash, numeric signs, figure and
table determinism, semantic validation, LaTeX builds, warning scans, flat
package integrity, and byte-identical ZIP replay. A fresh review receives only
the revised manuscript, supplement, canonical map/chronology, and closest-work
materials.
