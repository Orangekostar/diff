## Global wording constraints

Use `downstream-predictor-conditioned task value`. Do not use `intrinsic
mechanical value of a location`. The packages do not establish scanner-time
reduction, industrial deployment, external generalization, through-thickness
damage recovery, or a causal failure mechanism at selected C-scan locations.
