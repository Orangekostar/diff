# Transfer Conditions

The hierarchy can be instantiated beyond the current CFRP case study only when
the sensing problem supplies all five methodological conditions:

1. A defined downstream engineering endpoint and loss.
2. A legal partial-observation state that excludes future measurements and outcomes.
3. A defensible retrospective or counterfactual target for marginal information value.
4. Matched controls that separate measurement content from acquisition geometry and history.
5. An end-to-end cost-constrained decision metric distinct from local value prediction.

These are requirements for evaluating the three information claims. They are
not evidence of universal empirical transfer, external replication, or
cross-material validation. Empirical support remains bounded to the six
held-out experimental domains in the present data program.

The Pascoe--Pimenta--Pinho interlocked thin-ply publication and Zenodo dataset
provide independent literature context that a change in scalar damage extent
and a change in CAI performance need not be equivalent. The Rhead--Hua--Butler
publication provides a second literature-only example. Neither is analyzed as
an external replication of this hierarchy.
