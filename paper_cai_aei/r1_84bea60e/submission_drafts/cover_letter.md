Surface information and currently acquired C-scan descriptors inform sequential choices, while all compared policies share one frozen CAI predictor. Offline replay on 50 selected validation specimens characterizes prediction quality under common acquisition caps, empirical matched-quality requirements and signed timing contributions from saved trajectories.

The study connects engineering information acquisition with residual-strength assessment. It reports mixed component comparisons and a remaining complete-input quality gap. It does not claim independent specimen confirmation or physical inspection-time savings. We believe this bounded methodological contribution is relevant to research on task-driven inspection and engineering assessment.
