\section{S1. Evidence authority and statistical units}

The supplement exposes detailed controls, sensitivities, implementation
diagnostics, and machine-readable provenance omitted from the compressed main
paper. Numerical authority is the 42-row canonical metrics file. Physical
specimens are the first statistical unit and the six held-out experimental
domains are equally weighted. State, checkpoint, candidate, seed, and learner-
pair rows are repeated computational records rather than independent samples.
All teacher, oracle, and component-substitution quantities are retrospective
unless an evidence record explicitly marks them deployable.

% Reference code identity: mvd_m1_o2.
The codebase refers to the supervised state-conditioned closed-loop
implementation as MAVIS. This is one implementation of State-Conditioned
Task-Oriented Acquisition, not the paper-level method. The code identifier
\texttt{mvd\_m1\_o2} denotes the static deployable reference used in the final
comparison; it is not a published competing method.

\section{S2. Spatial information and sparse recoverability}

% Supplement-only evidence: U1_INDEPENDENT_FIELD_SENSITIVITY.
The registered main comparison uses the matched B-family scalar-to-spatial
path. The distinct independent internal-field estimator
(\texttt{U1\_INDEPENDENT\_FIELD\_SENSITIVITY}) had point MAE 0.08964 and is
retained only as a sensitivity path. It is not substituted for the matched
32.1\% effect.

The sparse condition observes a registered 25\% normalized-raster pattern and
uses bilinear interpolation before the frozen field pathway. Its 89.9\%
retention is referenced to the matched full-field gain. The residual sparse-to-
full MAE gap remains positive in all domains, and the sampling fraction has no
scanner-time interpretation.

\section{S3. Task-conditioned and predictor-conditioned value}

The main paper uses the preregistered \texttt{appearance\_oracle} as its
task-agnostic C-scan appearance-saliency reference. It assigns a candidate the mean
absolute RGB deviation of newly revealed samples from the specimen-specific
border median, normalized by 255, and uses no CAI outcome. Complete candidate
values make it retrospective and non-deployable. The registered protocol
predates formal oracle execution; the comparator is not a damage-severity
measure or a universal saliency definition. Appearance minus mechanical CAI
AUEBC was 0.0070800594 (95\% CI 0.0047993566--0.0097402930), with the CAI oracle
favored in all six domains. Across 276 paired initial-state maps, mean Spearman
agreement was 0.0222120 and mean top-decile overlap was 0.2003106.

% Supplement-only evidence: U3_RECONSTRUCTION_ORACLE;
% U4_ORACLE_CAI_SPECIFICITY; U4_ORACLE_IMAGE_SPECIFICITY;
% U4_LEARNED_SPECIFICITY_BOUNDARY.
Legacy reconstruction evidence is distinct from appearance saliency and is
retained here without relabeling. The one-shot mechanical oracle had CAI AUEBC
0.013458 versus 0.017188 for the legacy reconstruction oracle; the mechanical
improvement was 0.00373 (95\% CI 0.00284--0.00469), favorable in all six
domains (\texttt{U3\_RECONSTRUCTION\_ORACLE}). In the later registered
cross-objective diagnostic, the CAI-task oracle improved CAI AUEBC by 0.04862
(95\% CI 0.04527--0.05205)
(\texttt{U4\_ORACLE\_CAI\_SPECIFICITY}), while the reconstruction oracle
improved normalized RGB MSE by $5.503\times10^{-4}$ (95\% CI
$5.006\times10^{-4}$--$6.063\times10^{-4}$)
(\texttt{U4\_ORACLE\_IMAGE\_SPECIFICITY}). Source-trained global mechanics and
reconstruction masks produced the predeclared support indicator 0, so the
learned global mechanics mask did not reproduce that legacy oracle separation
(\texttt{U4\_LEARNED\_SPECIFICITY\_BOUNDARY}). These results remain bounded to
the registered normalized-RGB-MSE objective.

% Supplement-only evidence: U5_RIDGE_HUBER_BEST_ACTION;
% U5_RIDGE_HUBER_TOPK; U5_RIDGE_MLP_BEST_ACTION; U5_RIDGE_MLP_TOPK.
Under identical strict-OOF states and splits, Ridge--Huber best-action agreement
was 0.675 (95\% CI 0.622--0.727)
(\texttt{U5\_RIDGE\_HUBER\_BEST\_ACTION}), and top-$k$ agreement was 0.685
(95\% CI 0.650--0.719) (\texttt{U5\_RIDGE\_HUBER\_TOPK}). Ridge--MLP
best-action agreement was 0.233 (95\% CI 0.182--0.283)
(\texttt{U5\_RIDGE\_MLP\_BEST\_ACTION}), and top-$k$ agreement was 0.213
(95\% CI 0.192--0.235) (\texttt{U5\_RIDGE\_MLP\_TOPK}). Because the shallow
MLP was substantially less accurate, these contrasts do not isolate predictor
architecture at equal predictive accuracy.

\section{S4. State-conditioned valuation and source controls}

% Supplement-only evidence: O1_STATIC_SET_REGRET; O1_GLOBAL_SET_REGRET;
% O1_RANDOM_SET_REGRET.
The registered exact-budget set regrets were 0.08171 for the static scorer
(\texttt{O1\_STATIC\_SET\_REGRET}), 0.07993 for the global score
(\texttt{O1\_GLOBAL\_SET\_REGRET}), and 0.07978 for the random median
(\texttt{O1\_RANDOM\_SET\_REGRET}). These source-only references annotate the
static representation; they do not establish information-theoretic
unobservability.

% Supplement-only evidence: O3_FULL_FIELD_RECOVERY.
At the endpoint, the real-state trajectory recovered 26.6\% (95\% CI
10.6\%--41.0\%) of the static-to-independent-full-field error ratio
(\texttt{O3\_FULL\_FIELD\_RECOVERY}). Its denominator is the independent
internal-field sensitivity estimator, not the matched B-family field path.

The acquired-position/history control inherits the exact positions, ordering,
and costs of the recipient trajectory while removing measured content. The
shuffled-content control preserves recipient positions and budget while
sourcing values from separated donor content.

% Supplement-only evidence: O3_REAL_MINUS_RECONSTRUCTION.
An additional legacy reconstruction-derived control retains the frozen
\texttt{reconstruction} mode and registered normalized-RGB-MSE objective on the
same legal trajectory. Real minus this control was 0.03419 in CAI MAE (95\% CI
0.02450--0.04384), with real content favorable in one of six domains
(\texttt{O3\_REAL\_MINUS\_RECONSTRUCTION}). This technical control is not the
appearance-saliency comparator and was not relabeled as saliency. These control
semantics are required when interpreting the adverse real-minus-control
directions.

\section{S5. Cost-constrained realization and implementation diagnostics}

The reachable-set analysis is restricted to the registered two-action pool at
6.25\%. The joint near-oracle compares reachable sets retrospectively; it is
not deployable and does not establish arbitrary-horizon planning regret.

% Supplement-only evidence: A3_FEEDBACK_BENEFIT.
For \texttt{A3\_FEEDBACK\_BENEFIT}, the no-feedback reference retained an
AUEBC advantage of $1.496\times10^{-5}$ (95\% CI
$1.064\times10^{-5}$--$1.944\times10^{-5}$). With the registered sign
convention, feedback benefit was $-1.496\times10^{-5}$ (95\% CI
$-1.944\times10^{-5}$ to $-1.064\times10^{-5}$), with feedback favorable in
two of six held-out domains. This is a frozen implementation diagnostic and is
not generalized to other state representations or policies.

% Full system diagnostic: A4_BASELINE_MINUS_MAVIS.
For \texttt{A4\_BASELINE\_MINUS\_MAVIS}, equal-domain CAI AUEBC was 0.125053
for the supervised state-conditioned implementation and 0.124992 for the
static reference. Reference minus learned was
$-6.114\times10^{-5}$ (95\% CI $-8.461\times10^{-5}$ to
$-3.777\times10^{-5}$), and the learned implementation improved two of six
held-out domains. The direction favors the reference. This comparison is one
system-level endpoint and does not define the performance of the full Task-
Relevant Information Acquisition framework.

\section{S6. Provenance, chronology, and scope boundaries}

The evidence chronology separates pre-existing spatial and static-reference
analyses, the frozen outer implementation endpoint, and later diagnostic
analyses. Later diagnostics reused fixed states, source bindings, splits, and
outcomes; they were not used to select or modify the frozen endpoint. No model
training, tuning, result selection, frozen scientific artifact, or existing
canonical numerical value was changed. The controlled authority migration adds
three claims from preregistered frozen appearance evidence and changes only
paper visibility and narrative roles for legacy reconstruction claims.

The machine-readable visibility map assigns every canonical claim to main
headline, main support, one main system diagnostic, or supplement-only use.
Source-hash records bind every number to its immutable result artifact, and the
supplement manifest independently records copied-file digests. External-data
records are feasibility audits only and contain no external method-performance
evaluation.

Scope remains the paired 276-specimen, six-domain CFRP data program; exact
native-raster digital cost; the fixed predictor families, state
representations, action hierarchy, and planners; and the registered CAI and
appearance-saliency comparison. Legacy evidence retains the registered CAI and
normalized-RGB-MSE tasks. Physical path length, coupling, settling, hardware
timing, industrial cost, and external empirical transfer are not established.
