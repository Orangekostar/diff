\section{Purpose and statistical units}

This supplement exposes the audit-heavy records behind the main manuscript.
It does not introduce a second method, new training, or additional performance
claims. Physical specimens are the first statistical unit and the six held-out
experimental domains are the second. Repeated state, checkpoint, action, and
learner-pair rows are computational records rather than independent samples.
All oracle, teacher, and component-substitution records are retrospective and
non-deployable unless a file explicitly marks a row as deployable.

\section{Usefulness evidence details}

S01 provides all domain-level full-field candidate metrics, including the
matched metadata-plus-surface B-family path and the distinct independent
metadata-only I-family estimator. The registered confirmatory claim uses the
B-family scalar-to-spatial contrast. S12--S14 preserve the full planning and
learner-sensitivity records used to show that measurement value is conditioned
on the downstream predictor and decision set rather than being an intrinsic
property of a location.

\section{Observability controls}

S02 contains the full static-observability comparison. S03--S04 contain every
tested checkpoint transition and value source for the conditional teacher.
S05--S07 expose all state-matched content controls, including the unfavorable
position-only and reconstruction comparisons. S08--S09 retain the secondary
rank metrics and the shuffled-content adverse control. These records do not
support a general claim that specimen-specific measurement content is
observable from the tested legal state.

\section{Actionability and feedback diagnostics}

S10--S11 expose the full component-substitution matrix and domain effects. S12
contains the bounded two-action reachable-pool diagnostics; the retrospective
near-oracle is not a deployable policy and does not establish arbitrary-horizon
planning regret. S15--S16 retain the full feedback strata and association
analyses. They do not support conditional-value evolution as the operative
mechanism for improved feedback, and the main endpoint remains adverse.

\section{Provenance and external-data boundary}

S17 binds the paper-specific canonical authority to the immutable source
artifacts. `SUPPLEMENTARY\_DATA\_MANIFEST.csv' independently binds each copied
supplement file to its source digest. S18 is an audit of external dataset
feasibility only. It contains no external replication, scanner-time validation,
or industrial deployment result.
