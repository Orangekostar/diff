# P16 Feedback Mechanism Diagnostic

Frozen feedback does not show reliable benefit concentrated in specimens with larger real conditional-value changes; the diagnostic does not support feedback as the operative mechanism.

The frozen equal-domain feedback benefit is -1.4962762e-05 CAI AUEBC, where positive means feedback is better. Its synchronized 95% interval is [-1.9435185744e-05, -1.0636813205e-05]. No model was retrained. P4 feedback/no-feedback outcomes are joined to P9 real-scorer and teacher value-change diagnostics at the physical-specimen level.

Tertile probabilities were frozen before outcome analysis and assigned within held-out domains using only the mechanism variable; specimen ID breaks exact ties. These are descriptive post-hoc associations over six historical domains, not causal or external-confirmation evidence. P7 remains unchanged.
