# MVD M0 One-Shot Oracle Feasibility

Decision: `MVD_ONE_SHOT_STRONG_GO`.

The initial one-shot plan retains `0.568` of the stronger-baseline-to-sequential-oracle headroom. Its equal-domain P-B AUEBC is `0.01345794`, versus uniform `0.01736346`, reconstruction `0.01718766`, and sequential oracle `0.01062453`.

Uniform and reconstruction effects improve in `6/6` and `6/6` domains; both synchronized bootstrap lower bounds are positive. Exact candidate costs are unequal, so all plans use frozen ranking with exact unique-location fit/skip selection.

The interaction audit covers 1,380 strict source-OOF specimen-folds. For mechanical top-value sets, Pearson association is only about 0.21-0.28 at the intermediate checkpoints and additive gain overstates joint gain by about 0.048-0.072 on average. Interaction/non-additivity is therefore material, but descriptive and non-gating. M0 authorizes the minimum M1 observability test, not a deployable MVD claim.
